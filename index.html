How to use it

Why a card helps

It works without unlocking

Anyone helping you can see who to call and what to know, without guessing your passcode.

Nothing leaves your device

The card is drawn in your browser. No accounts, no uploads, no tracking — and it works offline.

You choose what is shown

Every field is optional. A privacy check flags details that are risky to display in public.

About me

How it works

Three steps, then you can put your phone down.

  1. Write what matters

    A name, one or two people to call, blood type and allergies. Skip anything you would not want a stranger to read.

  2. Pick a look

    Four high-contrast themes, sized for a lock screen or a wallet. Everything is drawn at print quality.

  3. Save and set it

    Save the image, then set it as your lock screen. Or print the sheet, cut it out and keep it in your wallet.

A lock screen is public to anyone holding your phone

Only include what you are comfortable showing a stranger. Never add a home address, full date of birth, ID or card numbers. Keep your official medical records up to date too.

It works alongside your phone

Most phones have a built-in emergency or Medical ID screen. This card is a simple backup you control — use both if you can.

Ready in three minutes.

Your details stay in your browser. You can change or delete them at any time.
