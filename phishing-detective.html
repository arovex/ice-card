Quick Reference

Phishing Cheat Codes

Memorize these red flags and you'll spot most attacks instantly.

Top Red Flags

Always Check First

01
URL Inspection Look for misspellings, extra hyphens, subdomains, or lookalike TLDs.
02
Sender Address Display names can lie. Always verify the actual email/domain.
03
Urgency & Pressure "Act now!", countdowns, and threats are classic manipulation.
04
Attachments & Links Beware of double extensions (.pdf.exe) and shortened/lookalike links.
05
Unusual Requests Legit orgs never ask for passwords, OTPs, gift cards, or crypto via email/SMS.
Pro Tips

Stay Safe

Hover Before Clicking Preview the destination URL without opening it.
Go Direct Type the URL or use the official app instead of email links.
Enable MFA Multi-factor auth is your best defense against stolen creds.
When in Doubt Verify through official channels before taking action.
Cybersecurity 101

Phishing Awareness

Understand the tactics, types, and defenses to stay ahead of social engineering.

Overview

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where attackers impersonate trusted organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data. It's one of the most common and effective attack vectors.

Attack Flow

How It Works

01
Reconnaissance Attackers research targets via social media, data breaches, or public info.
02
Crafting the Lure Creating convincing emails, SMS, or pages designed to trigger action.
03
Delivery Sent via email, SMS (smishing), calls (vishing), or social media.
04
Execution Victim clicks link or opens attachment and enters credentials/data.
05
Exploitation Stolen data used for account takeover, fraud, or further attacks.
Variants

Types of Phishing

Email Phishing Mass emails sent to thousands of recipients.
Spear Phishing Highly targeted using personal or org-specific details.
Whaling Targets executives and high-value individuals.
Smishing SMS/text message based phishing attacks.
Vishing Voice calls impersonating support or authorities.
Defense

Protection Checklist

Verify Everything Check URLs, senders, and requests through official channels.
Enable MFA Everywhere Use authenticator apps, not SMS when possible.
Use Password Manager Prevents autofill on lookalike domains.
Keep Updated Update OS, browsers, and security software regularly.
Report Suspicious Report to your IT/security team or provider.
