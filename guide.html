How to use it

How to use your card

Two places work best: the lock screen and a wallet card. Here is the whole process, including how to get back to your normal wallpaper later.

Lock screen

Set it as your lock screen

This is the version most people see first. Keep it on your phone’s lock screen and the card is visible without unlocking.

  1. On this site, choose Lock screen, then Save as image.
  2. Save the PNG to your photos. The top 30% stays clear for the clock, and the bottom 12% for the flashlight and camera buttons.
  3. iPhone: Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos → choose the card. Turn off depth effect and “Perspective zoom” so the text stays sharp.
  4. Android: Photos → the card → More → Set as wallpaper → Lock screen. Choose “no motion” if offered.
  5. Check it once with the screen locked: can you read the name and the first phone number without squinting?

Show safe zones

To go back, set your original photo as the wallpaper again. Your normal wallpaper is not deleted by this card.

Wallet

Print a wallet card

Paper does not run out of battery. A folded card also helps if your phone is damaged or locked away from you.

  1. Choose Wallet, check the front and back, then Print sheet.
  2. In the print dialog choose A4, 100% scale, and turn off headers and footers.
  3. Cut along the dashed lines. Put the front and back of a pair back to back, then trim the corners.
  4. A5 or Letter also works — the sheet scales, but check the cut lines before cutting.

Print sheet

Print two copies: one for your wallet, one for a family member or your bag.

Tip: Sunrise and Clinic use the least ink. Print at 100% scale, without "fit to page".

Print sheet

Print the family sheet

The print sheet lays out four front-and-back pairs on one A4 page, so you can give copies to family members.

  1. Fill in the card once, then choose Print sheet.
  2. Print at 100%. Each pair is credit-card sized (85.6 × 54 mm).
  3. Cut out each pair, fold or glue front to back, and write the person’s name on the back if you like.
  4. Laminate or use a clear sleeve for a card that survives a wallet.

Emergency contact cards

For a PDF, use “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. It is the same page, with no extra software.

Use your phone’s built-in features too

iPhone has Medical ID (Health app → Medical ID) and Android has Emergency information (Settings → Safety & emergency). They can be shown from the lock screen and can include emergency calls. This card is a simple backup — use both if you can.

Common questions

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is drawn in your browser. There is no server, no account and no analytics. The only copy is the one you save yourself.

What if I lose the printed card?

Nothing is stored on the paper except what you chose to print. Make a new one in three minutes, or keep a photo of the card in your bag.

Will the text be readable on a small phone?

The lock-screen layout leaves the top 30% for the clock and the bottom 12% for the camera and flashlight. Text is at least 28 px at that size. Check it once on your own phone before relying on it.

Can I make cards for my family?

Yes — the print sheet lays out four pairs. For different people, fill in the form again and print a second sheet, or save separate PNGs.
