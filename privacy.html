Privacy

Privacy, in plain language

This tool is built to keep your information on your own device. Here is exactly what happens, without legal language.

Works offline · No account · No tracking

What is stored, and where

The card is drawn by your browser. If you tick “Remember on this device”, the form is saved in this browser’s local storage — a small private storage area on your device. If you do not tick it, the form lives in memory only and disappears when you close the tab.

  • Your form fields, stored only if you ask us to remember them.
  • Your theme, sound and language choice, so the site feels the same next time.
  • A saved PNG, only where you save it yourself (photos or downloads).

What never happens

There is no backend to send anything to.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No network request carries your card details. The only network request is for optional fonts, which load from Google Fonts without your card data.
  • No logs of what you typed.

Remember on this device

This is off by default. Turn it on only on a device you trust. On a shared or work device, leave it off and the form is forgotten when the tab closes. You can delete everything with one button.

Remember on this device
Saves this form in your browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Leave it off on a shared device.

A lock screen is visible to anyone holding your phone

That is the point — but it also means a stranger can read it. Only include what you are comfortable showing. Do not add a home address, a full date of birth, ID numbers, card numbers or passwords. Use a landmark or a partial detail instead.

A convenience, not a medical record

This card is a convenience, not a medical record. Keep your official records up to date with your doctor, and use your phone’s built-in Medical ID or emergency information as well.

Works offline

Once the page has loaded, everything works without a connection. Fonts fall back to fonts already on your device. You can also save the page to your device and open it later.

Delete everything

This removes the saved form, theme, sound and language settings from this browser. It cannot be undone, and it does not affect any image you already saved.

Ready in three minutes.

Your details stay in your browser. You can change or delete them at any time.
